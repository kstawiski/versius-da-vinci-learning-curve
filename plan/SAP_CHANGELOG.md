All changes below were made on 25 September 2026 before any outcome was examined against case order. The only outcome-related output inspected before locking was completeness and documentation format by platform, hospital and year (scripts 00 to 02).

- AGY proposed a proficiency definition against an external pT2 margin benchmark below 15%. The benchmark is used only in LC-CUSUM; the plateau rule stays internal to the curve.
- muse-free proposed a calendar-month spline in every mature model. Within a platform this is collinear with case number, so calendar adjustment is used only in the 2024 concurrent comparison.

## Implementation notes during coding, before outcome inspection

- Robust variance. Linear-model contrasts use HC3 standard errors. When a leverage-1 observation (a covariate level with a single case inside a small contrast subset) makes HC3 undefined, HC1 is used for that fit. Logistic contrasts use HC0 with the same fallback.
- Imputation. Operative time and margin status are imputed as auxiliary variables inside the imputation model so that every covariate row can be completed. Imputed outcome values are never used as outcomes.

## Post hoc additions after outcome inspection (labelled post hoc wherever reported)

- 25 September 2026, after the primary run. The prespecified plateau rule (curve within 15 minutes of the mean of the final 50 cases for every later case) proved unstable. For Versius operative time it gave n* = 111 with a bootstrap interval of 32 to 289, and 40% of replicates never met it. The independent reproduction found the same (interval 35 to 337). The cause is a transient rise around Versius cases 75 to 110 and a dip in the final cases, where the programme was winding down. The rule's result is reported unchanged. A post hoc description of the steep phase is added from the same standardized curves with its own bootstrap: first entry into the 15-minute band, the case by which 80% and 90% of the initial improvement was achieved, and the fitted decline from case 1 to case 50 (`scripts/06_posthoc_learning_metrics.R`).
- Margin reporting. Structured margin-length reporting at Bełchatów rose from 12 to 29% of reports in 2022 to 2023 to 100% from 2024, coinciding with a rise in the all-stage positive-margin rate there from 9% to 35%. Post hoc descriptive tables by hospital and calendar half-year are added to the supplement.
- Sparse binary outcomes (implementation amendment after the primary run exposed separation). With 3 and 3 PSA-persistence events in the 2024 comparison, the full covariate set separated and the delta-method interval collapsed (reported estimate -9.5 points with a 95% interval of -10.9 to -8.1, an artefact). Binary contrasts in which either group has fewer than 10 events now use Firth penalized logistic regression adjusted for age, log PSA and hospital, with a marginally standardized risk difference (`scripts/03b_sparse_binary.R`). Zero-event outcomes are reported crude. The independent reproduction had already resolved the same case with Firth regression and parsimonious adjustment.
- Continence reporting. The prespecified endpoint stays pad-free (zero pads). Use of no pad or one safety pad at 12 months is added as a descriptive secondary definition because it is the definition most published series report.
- Margins by hospital, robot and calendar period (post hoc, after the storyline adversary review). Table 3 and unadjusted Newcombe-interval contrasts within hospital: Salve Medica restart January 2024 to June 2025 against Salve Medica Versius 2021 to 2023, and afterwards; Bełchatów Versius 2024 against Bełchatów Versius 2021 to 2023; Bełchatów restart against Bełchatów Versius 2024 (`scripts/10_table3_margins.py`).

## Amendments after the two-family methods and implementation review (Astra and Grok, 25 September 2026)

Both reviewers returned fail with overlapping findings. All accepted findings were implemented and the full pipeline was rerun (`scripts/run_all.sh`). Reviews: `delegated_tasks/ms6-methods-review-20260925/REVIEW_openai.md` and `REVIEW_xai.md`.

- Plateau bootstrap intervals now keep replicates in which the rule is never met as probability mass beyond the last case. The upper limit is reported as "not reached" when that share exceeds 2.5%. Conditional intervals are labelled as such.
- Mature-phase comparison (B1). The undocumented fallback to the final 50 Versius cases was removed. A bootstrap that re-selects both plateau cutoffs in every replicate quantifies cutoff uncertainty (`scripts/03c_mature_boot.R`). A post hoc fixed-cutoff comparison (Versius after case 46, the upper bootstrap limit of the segmented breakpoint, against all restart cases) is added. The comparison is described as era-confounded and conditional, never as platform equivalence.
- Segmented regression is fitted in all 20 imputations with multiple starting values, pooled with Rubin's rules; failed bootstrap fits are counted; an unadjusted breakpoint is added.
- One authoritative contrast table (`scripts/11_authoritative_contrasts.py`) selects the full model, the Firth model (only when either group has fewer than 10 events or no non-events) or crude counts. Every figure, table and text number reads it. Grade III or higher complications and transfusion are exact counts only, and blood loss is descriptive only, as prespecified.
- Team-curve milestones are indexed by the hospital team's case number. Joint surgeon-and-team model tests are not reported because the correlation exceeds 0.8; the restart-only correlation is added.
- Every curve interval uses posterior draws from all 20 imputations (mixture simulation).
- CUSUM recursions start at zero, like the threshold simulations. The risk-adjusted CUSUM uses the Steiner score for a doubling of the odds of a positive margin (odds ratio 2, the conventional choice; the plan did not state it). LC-CUSUM reports achieved alpha and beta at the observed series length.
- Imputation includes length of stay, blood loss, continence and PSA persistence as auxiliary variables. Imputed outcome values are never analysed.
- The pT2 analysis set is fixed to observed pathological stage. Imputed stage enters only as a covariate.
- Tie sensitivity permutes only unresolved order within 8 tie blocks (16 operations, 4 robotic) across all 838 operations, keeps resolved start-time order, rebuilds all dependent case numbers and holds imputed covariates fixed.
- The post hoc improvement fraction is undefined when there is no initial improvement; no tolerance override.
- Risk-model calibration is reported as observed against expected margins by quintile of predicted risk with an optimism-corrected AUC; in-sample calibration identities are not reported.
- Data close is read from the source last-contact date field instead of being reconstructed from months.
- PSA-persistence inverse-probability weighting and the Table 3 Newcombe contrasts are scripted.
- Figure 3 shows the post hoc comparison after the Versius learning phase (Versius cases 47 to 337, the upper bootstrap limit of the prespecified segmented breakpoint, against all restart cases) instead of the plateau-defined mature phases. The plateau-defined comparison depends on cutoffs that were not reached in 44% of bootstrap replicates; with cutoffs re-selected, its pT2 margin interval (90% interval -20.2 to 9.8 points) did not support equivalence. It is reported in Table S1.

## Cutoff provenance correction and cutoff sensitivity (25 September 2026, after the final v4 readings)

- The fixed cutoff for the post hoc comparison after the Versius learning phase (Versius after case 46) was set from the upper bootstrap limit of the first segmented fit (95% CI 19–46). The methods-review amendment pooled the segmented fit over 20 imputations, which narrowed that interval to 19–30. Case 46 coincides with the unadjusted breakpoint (46.0). A blinded whole-product reader found that manuscript v4 still called case 46 "the upper bootstrap limit". The cutoff is kept unchanged to avoid results-driven reselection, and the text now describes it accurately.
- POST HOC. `scripts/03d_cutoff_sensitivity.R` repeats the comparison with cutoffs at case 30 (upper limit of the pooled bootstrap interval) and case 43 (90% of the initial improvement). Output `analysis/results/B1f_cutoff_sensitivity.json`. Operative time −2.3 and −1.9 min (equivalent within ±15 min), pT2 margins +9.0 and +9.3 points. The case-46 rerun reproduces the original estimate exactly. Reported in Table S1 and Appendix S1.
- Continence label corrected. The secondary definition is at most one ordinary pad per day (`pads_12m <= 1`). The release counts ordinary pads and keeps safety pads as a separate device measure, so the label "one safety pad" was inaccurate.

## Revision after external pre-submission review (25 September 2026, manuscript v11)

- The prespecified A3a first-50-case model (series by case-number interaction) had been computed but not reported. It is now reported: difference at case 1, 106 min (95% CI 71–141); slopes −15.9 min per 10 cases on Versius and −10.3 on the restart (95% CI −18.1 to −2.6); slope difference 5.6 (95% CI −6.6 to 17.8). The prespecified A3c comparison (platform-specific versus cumulative robotic case number, AIC −185 against −168) is also reported.
- The smooth-term P values had been summarized as the median across imputations. `scripts/03e_pooled_smooth_tests.R` pools them by the D2 rule (restart pT2 margin curve P = 0.085).
- POST HOC. `scripts/03f_overlap_2024.R` tabulates 2024 operations by hospital and month and restricts the 2024 comparison to hospital-quarters with both robots (55 of 204 operations; operative time +9.0 min, 95% CI −7.5 to 25.5). It also reports observed and eligible denominators for functional outcomes and complication-grade and readmission ascertainment.
- The prespecified 2024 functional contrasts (A4) and the observation-weighted sensitivity (B5) disagreed in direction for 12-month pad-free status (Firth −12.3 points, weighted +18.1 points), with recording in 18 of 99 Versius and 42 of 105 restart operations. Functional outcomes are therefore reported descriptively, and the functional contrast figure is withdrawn.

## Source-record completion (25 September 2026, manuscript v13)

- Reoperation adjudication. The six recorded Versius reoperations were classified from the registry reoperation cell and reason text by a transparent keyword rule (surgical, endoscopic or radiological reintervention for a complication is Clavien-Dindo grade III by definition; planned or oncological procedures are not complications). Of the two graded below III, one was a planned oncological procedure and one had no recorded indication. The grade III or higher rate is unchanged at 2%.
